# Marketplace copy

**Name:** URL Shortener by A.af

**Short description:** Create secure short links and QR codes, then view privacy-safe analytics in your A.af workspace.

**Description:** Connect your own A.af account to Kimi to create short links without leaving the conversation. Manage links in workspaces you can access, generate scannable QR codes, and review real aggregate analytics. A.af validates destination URLs and applies workspace permissions, plan limits, and Trust and Safety checks. Existing links may be reused under A.af's duplicate rules. The plugin does not promise custom domains or Business analytics to accounts whose plans do not include them.

**Example requests:**

- Shorten `https://example.com/launch?utm_source=kimi` with A.af.
- List the active links in my A.af workspace.
- Create a QR code for this A.af link.
- Show the last 30 days of analytics for my link.
- Pause this link temporarily; do not archive it.

**Permissions and privacy:** Users authorize A.af individually through OAuth. The plugin requests no shared API key and cannot bypass A.af workspace roles or plan limits. Link destinations and account-scoped records are sent only as needed to fulfill a user's request. Analytics returned by A.af are privacy-safe aggregates, not raw visitor IP addresses. Users can revoke access in A.af.
